The idea

A futures trade, expressed as a drawing.

Trace where you think SOL goes over the next minute, with your finger.

Stake

Score

Every second's move is checked against your line. Call the direction right more than half the time and you profit.

No order types, no liquidation price, no guessing where to click. One gesture, one minute, one number.

How a round is scored

Direction, second by second.

The round has 60 samples, one per second. At each step we compare which way your line moved with which way the real price moved. Steps where the price did not tick are skipped.

accuracy = right calls ÷ compared steps
PnL = (2 × accuracy − 1) × max profit
max profit = |price move| × stake ÷ start price × leverage

Loss is floored at your stake. Fee is 2% of profit only.

Built for Solana Mobile

The whole loop fits in a thumb and a minute.

Mobile Wallet Adapter 2.0

Connect, authorize and sign through the wallet already on the phone. The auth token is cached in secure storage and refreshed silently.

Sign here, submit from the app

The wallet only signs. The app checks what came back (only fee instructions may be added), then sends it through its own RPC.

Made for a small screen

A finger-drawn canvas, a slot-machine lever, haptics on every pull and result, and the screen stays awake during a round.

Wallet errors that make sense

Backing out of the wallet is a retry, not a failure. A fresh blockhash is fetched after approval so devnet's short expiry does not bite.

Trust model

What is checked, and what is still trusted.

Enforced on-chain

  • Only the settler key can close a position, and only after 60 seconds
  • PnL is bounded to between your stake lost and stake × leverage
  • The drawing's hash is stored before the round starts; a different drawing is rejected
  • Fee on profit only, pause switch, a vault that cannot go below rent

Trusted today

  • The settler records prices from a public exchange feed and scores the round
  • One settler key, held off-device
  • Emergency withdraw exists, as a demo-stage switch

Next: read prices from an on-chain oracle so the score can be checked by anyone.
